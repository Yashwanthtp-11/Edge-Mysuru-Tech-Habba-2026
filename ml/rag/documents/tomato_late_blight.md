# Late Blight

## Crop
Tomato

## Symptoms

- Water-soaked dark lesions on leaves and stems.
- Lesions can expand rapidly and cause necrosis.
- White sporulation may appear under suitable humid conditions.
- Fruits can develop dark brown lesions and rot.

## Favorable Conditions

- Cool nights, warm days, and prolonged wet conditions from rain or fog.

## Management

- Maintain proper drainage.
- Use crop rotation.
- Remove infected debris and manage disease sources.
- Follow current local extension recommendations for chemical control.

## Prevention

- Avoid prolonged leaf wetness where practical.
- Monitor weather conditions favorable to disease.
- Use healthy planting material and sanitation.
