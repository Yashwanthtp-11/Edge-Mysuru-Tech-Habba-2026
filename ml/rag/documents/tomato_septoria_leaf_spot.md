# Septoria Leaf Spot

## Crop
Tomato

## Symptoms

- Numerous small round-to-irregular leaf spots with gray centers and dark margins.
- Spots can merge and severely blight leaves.
- Severe infection can cause defoliation.
- Stems and flowers may also be affected.

## Favorable Conditions

- High humidity.
- The pathogen survives in infected plant debris.
- Rain splash and wind can spread spores.

## Management

- Remove and destroy affected plant parts.
- Reduce sources of infected debris.
- Use current local extension recommendations for additional control measures.

## Prevention

- Field sanitation.
- Avoid practices that prolong leaf wetness.
- Use healthy planting material and follow local extension guidance.
