# Cedar Apple Rust

## Crop
Apple

## Symptoms

- Yellow to bright-orange leaf spots, often with a reddish border.
- Small raised black structures can occur on the upper surface.
- The underside may develop fringed/tube-like structures.
- The disease involves apple/crabapple and a juniper/red-cedar alternate host.

## Favorable Conditions

- Rust infection requires prolonged leaf wetness.
- Windborne spores can move between alternate hosts.

## Management

- Use resistant apple cultivars where appropriate.
- Manage nearby alternate hosts where practical and locally appropriate.
- Disease-control timing should be based on local orchard conditions and extension guidance.
- Once visible rust lesions are established, fungicide control is generally too late for that infection cycle.

## Prevention

- Prefer resistant cultivars.
- Monitor both apple and nearby alternate hosts.
- Use local disease-forecasting/extension guidance for preventative management.
