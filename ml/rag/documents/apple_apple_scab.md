# Apple Scab

## Crop
Apple

## Symptoms

- Olive-green to brown irregular or feathered leaf spots that become dark brown to black.
- Infected leaves may yellow and drop prematurely.
- Fruit can develop raised corky spots and may become deformed or cracked.

## Favorable Conditions

- The pathogen overwinters in fallen infected leaves.
- Wet foliage is important for infection; infection periods depend on temperature and duration of leaf wetness.

## Management

- Reduce overwintering inoculum by managing fallen infected leaves.
- Use local disease-risk/forecast information.
- Fungicide timing should follow local extension guidance and current product labels.

## Prevention

- Plant scab-resistant cultivars where suitable.
- Maintain orchard sanitation.
- Improve canopy drying and airflow.
