# Black Rot

## Crop
Apple

## Symptoms

- Round leaf spots with a purple border and tan center.
- Fruit develops brown rot, often with concentric rings.
- Fruit may remain firm and become mummified.
- Branch cankers and dieback can occur.

## Favorable Conditions

- Tree stress increases susceptibility.
- Dead wood, cankers, mummified fruit, pruning wounds, and fire-blight-damaged tissue can act as inoculum sources.

## Management

- Prune out dead or diseased branches and cankers.
- Remove and dispose of mummified fruit and infected plant material.
- Maintain tree health and reduce stress.
- Chemical control should follow current local extension recommendations and product labels.

## Prevention

- Maintain orchard sanitation.
- Remove dead wood and mummified fruit.
- Manage fire blight and other sources of dead tissue.
- Maintain healthy, non-stressed trees.
