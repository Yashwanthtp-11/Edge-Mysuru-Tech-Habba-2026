# Early Blight

## Crop
Tomato

## Symptoms

- Brown leaf spots with concentric rings in a bull's-eye pattern.
- Yellowing may surround lesions.
- Fruit may develop brown concentric rings.
- Defoliation can expose fruit to sunscald.

## Favorable Conditions

- Warm, humid weather.
- Rain and wet conditions favor disease development.
- The pathogen survives in infected plant debris.

## Management

- Remove and destroy infected crop debris.
- Rotate crops with non-solanaceous crops.
- Use healthy seed.
- Use locally recommended disease-management products only according to current label and extension guidance.

## Prevention

- Field sanitation.
- Crop rotation.
- Reduce prolonged wetness in the crop canopy where practical.
